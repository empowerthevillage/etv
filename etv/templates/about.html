ETV's Mission
VISION
Restore Prosperity, Peace and Power to the communities we serve by prioritizing health, wealth, and empowerment.
MISSION
Empower The Village, Inc. (ETV) is a data-driven nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization founded in 2018 to develop, guide and implement breakthrough, scalable strategies that empower people, business owners, and community organizations to realize their full potential.
STRATEGIC IMPERATIVE
Apply our Strategic Partnerships Service Delivery Model and data driven solutions to accomplish our mission. ETV partners with the village - community-led organizations, subject matter experts, individual and corporate philanthropists - to accelerate results on achievable and mutually aligned goals that empower communities.

    Founder, CEO & President

    AYO SANDERSON WILSON

    The time for simply "admiring" problems, discussing root causes, and being satisfied with modest, incremental change to uplift our communities has passed, according to Ayo Sanderson Wilson.

    That's why she transitioned from a career as a successful senior business executive and attorney in Corporate America to make Empower The Village (ETV) a full-time commitment.

    Ayo founded ETV in 2018 to directly connect and collaborate with the communities ETV serves because it will "take a village" to accomplish ETV's mission - advancing economic prosperity and wellness by strategically partnering with Village leaders and allies to empower individuals, families, business owners and community organizations to realize their full potential.
