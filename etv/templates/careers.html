Why Join Our Team

Our Empower The Village vision is to restore Prosperity, Peace, and Power to the Black and historically marginalized communites. ETV's mission is to empower individuals, business owners, and community organizations to realize their full potential.

As a community-led, data-driven nonprofit, we are dedicated to making a tangible impact and fostering meaningful change with a focus on Wealth, Health, and Empowerment.

Becoming a part of ETV means joining a passionate team committed to uplifting the Black community. We value collaboration, innovation, and dedication. Our inclusive environment ensures that every voice is heard, and every team member contributes to our shared vision.

Current Opportunities
Director of Operations
The ETV Director of Operations is responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operational functions of the nonprofit organization. This role ensures the organization operates efficiently and effectively, aligning operational strategies with the mission and goals of the organization. They collaborate with various functions to implement best practices, manage resources, and enhance program delivery.
