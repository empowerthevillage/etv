Why Join Our Team

At Empower The Village, our mission is to restore Prosperity, Peace, and Power to the Black community by empowering individuals, businesses, and community organizations to realize their full potential. As a community-led, data-driven nonprofit, we are dedicated to making a tangible impact and fostering meaningful change.

Becoming a part of ETV means joining a passionate team committed to uplifting the Black community. We value collaboration, innovation, and dedication. Our inclusive environment ensures that every voice is heard, and every team member contributes to our shared vision.
