Participant Agreement & Program Consent

Empower The Village, Inc.
Village Empowerment Network (VEN)

Thank you for participating in the Village Empowerment Network (VEN), a business development program of Empower The Village, Inc. (“ETV”). By participating, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

1. Voluntary Participation
I understand that my participation in VEN is voluntary. I may withdraw from the program at any time by notifying ETV.

2. Nature of Services
VEN provides educational business coaching, mentoring, networking, workshops, and referrals. These services are not legal, tax, accounting, financial, investment, or other licensed professional advice. I understand that I am responsible for obtaining independent professional advice when appropriate.

3. Participant Responsibility & Release
I remain solely responsible for all business decisions and accept responsibility for actions I take or choose not to take based upon coaching or program resources. ETV makes no guarantee of business success or outcomes.

I acknowledge that any recommendations, coaching, suggestions, opinions, referrals, or educational information provided by ETV, its employees, contractors, coaches, mentors, volunteers, board members, partners, or guest speakers are offered in good faith as educational guidance only.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, I release and hold harmless ETV and its officers, directors, employees, volunteers, coaches, mentors, contractors, sponsors and partners from claims arising from my participation.

4. Surveys & Reporting
I understand that ETV relies on participant outcome data to evaluate program effectiveness, improve services, and demonstrate impact to funders, sponsors, and other stakeholders.

I agree to complete reasonable program surveys and follow-up reporting. ETV may use aggregated or de-identified information for evaluation, research, grant reporting and marketing.

5. Photo, Video, and Testimonial consent
I authorize ETV to use my name, likeness, voice, business name, photographs, video, audio, and testimonials during VEN activities in its website, social media, newsletters, annual reports, fundraising, grant proposals, presentations, media coverage, and other promotional materials.

6. Confidentiality
ETV will make reasonable efforts to protect confidential business information except where disclosure is required by law, audit, or grant compliance.

7. Professional Conduct
I agree that professional and respectful conduct is expected of all VEN participants, coaches, mentors, volunteers, and ETV staff. I will conduct myself professionally and respectfully throughout my participation in the program and will promptly notify ETV if I experience or become aware of conduct-related concerns.
