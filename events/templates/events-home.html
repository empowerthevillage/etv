Unity Walk for Community Wellness & Mental Health
May 16, 2026

South Mountain Reservation in West Orange, New Jersey, and virtually

In an effort to collectively promote community wellness and mental health, ETV is hosting a Walk on May 16, 2026 at South Mountain Reservation in West Orange, New Jersey. The "Unity Walk" is meant to bring together members of the Village who are invested in the physical and mental health of the community during Mental Health Awareness month. ETV is inviting the whole community, with emphasis on recruiting walking teams from church groups, Divine 9 chapters, civic and social organizations, books clubs, corporate employee groups, walking clubs and individuals to build community and benefit ETV's Village Assist Program and other wellness initiatives.

Data shows that regular physical exercise and time outdoors greatly improves mental and physical health and creates the perfect backdrop to exhale, experience the joy of nature, connect with others and feel inspired to build community. This event will take place from 8:00 AM until 12:00 PM with a vigorous 1.8 mile walk or 5k Fun Run around the lake (or virtually) with other fun activities and health education related resources shared! Check-in starts at 8:00 AM, and the walk begins at 9:00 AM. Take a walk with us and/or sponsor the event. We look forward to seeing you there!
